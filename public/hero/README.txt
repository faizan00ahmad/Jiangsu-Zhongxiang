Cinematic hero photos
=====================
Drop a wide photo (rider in a helmet, dark, motion blur, about 1920x1080) here, named after the bike's slug:

  r-xfx.jpg      -> hero slide for R-XFX
  r-jlong.jpg    -> hero slide for R-JLong
  retro-cg.jpg   -> hero slide for Retro-CG

(.jpg, .webp, .png and .avif work.) A photo here replaces the studio cut-out for that slide.
Put the bike toward the right and keep the left third dark: the model name and button sit there.
Only use photos you own or are licensed to use.
